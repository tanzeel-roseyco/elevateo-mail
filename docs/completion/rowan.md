## SEND-809 continuation candidate

It returns `{"allowed":false}` on any failed gate. `reconcile_no_evidence` closes a leased reconciliation step as durable unknown or, after five unknown checks, needs_review. A later accepted local observation must be recorded under a newly claimed reconcile lease for the original attempt, not under the stale lease.

# Product appearance

Elevateo Mail is a single light corporate application. White surfaces and black text keep dense mail workflows readable; gold outlines and focus indicators identify interactive regions. Statuses retain explicit words/icons and accessible red, amber or green accents. No dark theme is offered.

The existing navigation and task flows are preserved: read mail, edit drafts, explicitly review sends, track status, manage outreach, find addresses and configure the workspace. The design does not change permissions or enable live sending.

The authoritative palette, component rules and platform mapping are in [the design system](../design-system.md). Verification and platform limitations are in [the receipt](../completion/design-system-20261101/README.md).

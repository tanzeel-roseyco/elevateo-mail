## What these checks establish

They verify the fictional-data frontend and its persistence/navigation behaviour. They do not establish real provider connectivity, email delivery, production quota enforcement, authentication, tenant isolation, DNS permissions, AI access, native packaging or hosting cost. The daily-limit setting is capped at 30 and old valid saved settings migrate safely; a production send ledger is still required. The current mailbox-label shorthand is distinct for fixtures, not proven for arbitrary future domains.
